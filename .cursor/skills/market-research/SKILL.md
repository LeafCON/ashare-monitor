# Market research the monitor does not invent

The local app records prices, gates orders, and can fetch a few public crypto quotes. It does not install AgentKit, it does not rewrite the live schema by itself, and it does not invent fundamentals.

When this skill is active, do the research the app left open. Use only numbers you actually retrieved. Every figure needs a source and a date. If you do not have a number, write 未取得. Label estimates and speculation as such.

## Regime

From BTC and ETH trend, dominance, stablecoin liquidity, funding, open interest, and the macro notes the operator provides, pick one: trending, mean_reverting, high_vol, or crisis. Say what would flip that call.

## Setups

List 5 to 10 names only when you can point at a real disconnect between price and something you sourced (fees, revenue, TVL, users, unlock, or a dated catalyst). For each:

- What is confirmed, and what is speculation
- Whether value accrues to the token
- The bear case
- The evidence that would invalidate the idea
- Whether costs and slippage would eat the edge

## What you must not do

Do not place orders. Do not loosen the app's drawdown, daily-loss, position, or kill-switch limits. Do not claim a catalyst is unpriced without a source. End with a section titled WHAT COULD I BE WRONG ABOUT?
